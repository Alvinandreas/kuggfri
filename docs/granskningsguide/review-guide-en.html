Getting started

  1. 1

    Open the link

    Open the link from Alvin in your browser. It lets you in for a week while the service is closed to students.

  2. 2

    Create an account

    The sign-in page is in Swedish: choose Skapa konto (Create account) with the address Alvin registered, then confirm via email.

  3. 3

    Switch to English

    Turn on English at the top right of the review page. It applies to you only; students see Swedish.

  4. 4

    Open Review

    In the sidebar, under Administration, choose Review. Pick a topic under Topic and press Start reviewing.

One card at a time

The card is shown the way the student sees it, with the correct answer marked. The question to ask is the one at the top: is the content correct, and is it phrased the way you want?

  1. 1Move between cards with the arrows, or with J and K. Back to list takes you back.
  2. 2Topic, question type and sources. The line below says whether the card is new, an original card from earlier cohorts or a corrected original.
  3. 3Show Swedish shows the text the students actually see. A warning appears here if the Swedish card has changed since it was translated.
  4. 4Question and answer, as the student sees them. Answer options are marked correct or wrong.
  5. 5Sources expands to show exactly which lecture, page or exercise the card is based on.
  6. 6The action bar with your decisions. The letter on each button is its keyboard shortcut.

Four decisions

✓ Approve G

The card is correct and phrased the way you want. It moves to Reviewed and stays in rotation.

Flag F

Something needs to change. Write what in English and press Enter. We fix the Swedish card and its translation, and it comes back to you for approval.

Remove from rotation T

The card does not belong in the course. Students no longer see it, and it moves to Removed, where it can be put back.

Edit R

Opens the card’s Swedish text for editing. For wording changes, flagging with an English note is usually easier: we make the change in both languages.

Keyboard shortcuts

The letters come from the Swedish button names (G for Godkänn), so they are the same in both languages.

Good to know

  • Every decision is saved immediately and takes you to the next card. You can stop at any time and continue later.
  • Undo with Ctrl Z, or with Undo in the message after each decision.
  • Changed your mind? Open the card under Reviewed or Removed and press Mark as unreviewed (O).
  • If an approved card is changed, it returns to To review.

Flagged cards from Kuggfri’s source check

Before the review, every card was checked against the course’s own material: lectures, lab instructions, old exams and the course literature. Where the sources disagree, or where we have corrected an original card, the card carries a flag with a note on what was found and which decision is needed. 16 of your cards are flagged. They are listed under both To review and Flagged, with the note shown above the card.

Dealing with a flag

  • ✓ Approve and remove the flag GThe card is correct as it stands. The flag disappears and the card counts as reviewed.
  • Restore the originalUnder … on corrected original cards, when you prefer the original wording to our correction. The original is then approved.
  • ResolvedOnly removes the flag. The card stays under To review.
  • Change the noteAnswer us in the note with your decision or your question, in English, and we take it from there.
  • Remove from rotation TIf the card should not be in the course at all.

Flagging a card yourself

Press F, write what needs to be fixed and press Enter. You move straight on to the next card.

Where the cards end up

To reviewCards nobody has approved yet, flagged ones included.
ReviewedApproved cards, most recently reviewed first, with who approved them and when.
FlaggedAll cards with a flag, until the flag is resolved.
RemovedCards taken out of rotation. Students do not see them.

Working in English

  • The translations follow the terminology of your lectures and the course literature.
  • Students study the Swedish cards. Show Swedish on each card shows exactly what they see.
  • If a translation reads oddly, flag the card and tell us. That is useful too.

A suggested way of working

  • Take one topic at a time: choose it under Topic and press Start reviewing.
  • Approve what is correct right away; most cards go quickly.
  • When in doubt, flag rather than skip. Then we see the question.
